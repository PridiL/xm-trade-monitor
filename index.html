Pip value, margin & swap use XM's All-in-One calculator formulas and XM Standard-account specs, priced with live rates.

Pip = 10 × the instrument's last price digit (XM definition: 0.0001 for 5-digit pairs, 0.01 for JPY pairs, 0.1 for GOLD). Pip value only changes when the quote currency moves against USD — USD-quoted pairs stay fixed. "Δ prev close" compares with the pip value at yesterday's close; "Δ session" with the first value since this page was opened. Margin = lots × contract × base→USD ÷ leverage (XM leverage caps applied, e.g. 1:400 on CHF pairs, 1:50 on HKD/DKK/CNH).

Indices, oil and crypto are not in XM's calculator. Value per point was worked out from your own closed trades in the XM statement (GER40 €1, HK50 HK$1, JP225 ¥1, US indices $1, UK100 £1 per point per lot; Brent 100 bbl; BTC 1 coin). Prices come from the closest TradingView feed — cash-index and futures feeds can differ from XM's CFD by a few points, and feeds marked delayed lag 10–15 min.
Margin follows XM: lots × contract × price × (profit ccy→USD) ÷ min(account leverage, instrument leverage). Instrument leverage is XM's Standard-account maximum from xm.com (Equity indices, Energies, Crypto pages, read 18 Nov 2026): US30 / US100 / US500 / UK100 / GER40 / JP225 1:500, HK50 1:66.67, OILCash & BRENTCash 1:200, OIL & BRENT futures 1:66.67, BTC & ETH 1:1000. Open positions use their open price, as MT4 does. The estimate uses the TradingView price, so expect a small gap to MT4.

Defaults: GER40Cash=OANDA:DE30EUR, HK50Cash=OANDA:HK33HKD, JP225Cash=CME:NIY1!, BRENT & BRENTCash=FX:UKOIL, FX pairs=OANDA:<pair>.
Positions are saved in this browser only (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded anywhere: the page only downloads prices from TradingView's public scanner.
